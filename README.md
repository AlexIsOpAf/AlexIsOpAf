## What I believe

- **Rigor is the point** — I'd rather understand why something works than be surprised that it does. Clear contracts, correctness, and failure modes you've actually thought about.
- **The wrong prototype is worse than no prototype** — prototypes exist to teach you something, and one that dodges the hard concerns or can't reasonably scale teaches you the wrong lesson with confidence. Prototype the risk, not the demo.
- **Scale is a design input, not a milestone** — "we'll deal with it later" usually means "someone else will deal with it later."
- **Perfectionism is a tool** — aim it at what's expensive to change (data models, interfaces, the things that page you) and let everything else iterate.
- **The model is the smallest part of the system** — building agents is a distributed-systems problem: orchestration, state, retries, evals. That's exactly why I love it.
- **Competition keeps you honest** — in games the scoreboard doesn't care about your excuses. You're either cracked or not. Good engineering works the same way.
